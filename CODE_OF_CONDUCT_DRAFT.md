# Community Participation Covenant — DRAFT

This document is a proposal, not a finalized or operational moderation policy.

REGEN should welcome people across backgrounds and viewpoints who can work within a shared commitment to dignity, consent, evidence, and nonviolence. Disagreement over means and evidence is expected, and asking for clarification is welcomed.

## Proposed community standards

- Critique conduct, claims, rules, or institutions; do not dehumanize people.
- Do not harass, threaten, doxx, profile, intimidate, or incite retaliation.
- Never request covert access, unauthorized recording, impersonation, or the disclosure of confidential information.
- Do not treat political identity, social status, or follower counts as a proxy for someone’s moral worth.
- Give affected people a meaningful chance to challenge proposals and outcome claims.
- Accept correction, share uncertainty, and distinguish self-reported actions from independently evaluated outcomes.
- Respect different physical, economic, language, and communication needs.

## Processes to establish before accepting public reports

A maintainer roster, private reporting route, conflict-recusal procedure, interim safety holds, predictable review standards, appeal path, and data retention policy. Do not publish a contact address until it is staffed and secure.

No one is above the rules, including founders and funders. Policies should be developed with community input rather than declared finished at the prototype stage.
