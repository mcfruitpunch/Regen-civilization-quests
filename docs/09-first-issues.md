# 09 · Initial GitHub Backlog

This is a suggested roadmap. Issue creation and assignments should reflect maintainer capacity and the consent of collaborators.

## P0 · Safeguards and launch prerequisites

1. **Decide software/content licenses and ownership.** Define who can copy, redistribute, fork, and commercially use source code and creative content. Do not assume a public GitHub repository is automatically open-source.
2. **Review code of conduct, contributions, conflict policy, appeals.** Determine who receives reports, what authority they have, how the process is challenged, and how sensitive material stays out of public issues.
3. **Quest safety and independent-impact claims.** Establish structured review gates, explicit harm exclusions, uncertainty labels, and legitimate appeals by affected people.
4. **Consent-based community pilot.** Choose one well-bounded outcome and collaborate with one willing site or community group on a reversible test.

## P1 · Product and engineering

5. **Keyboard and screen-reader accessibility audit.** Test dialogs, focus, contrast, motion preferences, time estimates, and low-bandwidth behavior.
6. **Explainable quest recommendations.** Deterministic filtering by time, setting, skills, prerequisites, and participation mode. Explain every recommendation.
7. **Versioned schema and Atlas references.** Agree stable node identifiers and document what linked references mean, without presenting inferred relationships as facts.
8. **Private data export and reset.** Offer clear user control over demo progress and future consented records.
9. **Pilot measurement playbook.** Define baseline, measures, reporting period, uncertainty, independent review, adverse events, and corrections.

## P2 · Collaboration and community learning

10. **Accessible language and translations.** Co-design plain-language and multilingual quest formats.
11. **Bridge Protocol workshop.** Evaluate whether institutional invitations and firm accountability both work for affected stakeholders.
12. **Transparent public reporting template.** Include positive and negative outcomes, distribution of burden, participation attrition, disputes, and limitations.

## Suggested order

Prioritize license, governance, and safety decisions before expanding public contribution. Then validate usability and test a small opt-in pilot before investing in account systems or community dashboards.
