# License decision — OPEN

The v0.1 prototype and documents are provided for project evaluation. An open-source license has **not** been selected. The absence of a license does **not** grant others broad rights to copy, modify, distribute, or sublicense this code.

## Decision criteria

- Keep reusable civic tools available for local communities without vendor lock-in.
- Balance commercial adoption with safeguards against proprietary capture.
- Choose whether modifications to hosted services must be shared.
- Keep data/schema interoperability distinct from rights to software, artwork, name, and written content.
- Make contributor attribution and intellectual-property terms clear.

## Leading choices to evaluate

- **Apache-2.0:** Permissive reuse with express patent terms; proprietary derivatives permitted.
- **AGPL-3.0:** Strong network copyleft; modification-disclosure obligations can discourage some organizations.
- **Separate licenses:** Code, quest content, and documentation may merit different treatment.

**Next decision:** Project owner chooses licensing after reviewing legal and community trade-offs. Do not imply REGEN is open source until a license is added.
