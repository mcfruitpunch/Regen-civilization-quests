Quest Engine / Version 0.2

A route that fits your real life.

Choose only what matters to you. Suggestions are explainable and based on small, practical conditions—not identity, beliefs, personality scores, or hidden algorithms. All quests here are fictional examples, not approved real-world missions.

Why these quests?

The engine checks your selected preferences and which suggested steps you marked complete.

Preparation is advisory, not a barrier. Open any example quest at any time; completion is self-reported and does not verify skills or community outcomes.

Suggested next steps

Explore the full pathway

You can inspect, skip, or revisit any suggested preparation. Every route can be adapted for different abilities and resources.

Your progress belongs to you

No account or server. Only quest IDs you mark complete are stored locally in this browser (if storage is available). Export is optional; it contains no name, location, or evidence of impact.

Skill pathways

Different strengths. Shared possibility.

You don't have to become great at everything. These pathways help you notice what interests you. The small bars below reflect example quests you marked complete—never an objective measure of competence.

Progress is not a social score.

REGEN should never assign a moral value, civic credit rating, or access to public services based on activity. Real-world skills need evidence appropriate to the domain; the demo only tracks local participation.

Institutional transformation

Change the system. Keep the door open.

People are not enemies to defeat. Harmful actions can require consequences, and harmful institutions may need reform or lawful replacement. Invitation and accountability belong together.

Our operational promise

We will not use covert infiltration, deception, harassment, or social pressure to 'fix' unwilling individuals. We will support transparent dialogue, collective self-determination, lawful oversight, independent evidence, and protection of people harmed by existing practices.

Ten steps of the Bridge Protocol

Repair is proven through action.

Good intentions don't erase harm. Independent review, affected-community input, remediation, and durable results matter more than a sponsorship or public-relations badge.
