How this differs from proof

Activity

Someone records that an action happened. A self-report cannot establish quality, legality, safety or its effects.

Output

An artifact or service is produced. Its existence does not establish who benefited or whether it worked.

Outcome

A condition changes. Measurement design, confounders, comparison groups, uncertainty and harms still matter.

Accountability

Correction, withdrawal and independent challenge must remain possible. A publication badge is not purchased praise.
