    Pre-community exit gates

    • Independent governance and license decisions recorded.
    • Threat modeling, negative-case tests, privacy review, and accessibility testing with actual assistive technologies.
    • Safety, consent, revocation and complaints processes staffed and documented.

    • Outcomes distinguish invented model results, self-reports, and externally reviewed evidence.
