Review with care.

Practice receiving draft missions, identifying gaps and recording a transparent recommendation. The workbench cannot approve publication, validate a person's identity, or establish consent.

Local-only review. No file is uploaded. Do not inspect private dossiers, identifying allegations, or sensitive reports in this demonstration. Treat all author declarations as unverified. This is not an official authorized-review system.

1. Open a draft

No proposal loaded.

2. Review the risk and consent record

Import a local draft to see its safety declarations and fields.

3. Record a recommendation

You may suggest revision, reject an unsafe concept, or refer a thoroughly documented proposal for a *separate* authorized human review. There is no approval choice.

What this does not authorize

Completing a review worksheet does not approve, certify, publish, or authorize any quest. Future live signoff requires trusted reviewer identities, independence, governance, version binding, appeals and protected production controls. See the proposed approval protocol.
